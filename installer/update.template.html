Update Slay the Spire 2 tiers

Reads the Mobalytics tier list in your browser and hands it to the game. You only install the bookmark once.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar Update STS2 tiers
    Can't see the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Open the Mobalytics tier list Open Mobalytics
  3. Once it loads, click the "Update STS2 tiers" bookmark
    It downloads sts2-tiers-DATE.json and the game loads it within a few seconds. From now on, the in-game button opens Mobalytics directly: just click the bookmark.
